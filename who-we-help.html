Signs it might be time

Does any of this sound familiar?

  • Almost all of your new business comes from referrals and repeat customers.
  • You’re a little embarrassed to send people to your website.
  • Buyers have to call to find out basic things, like what you make or where you work.
  • There’s no clear way to request a quote or start a conversation online.
  • You know which kinds of customers you want more of, but nobody is going after them.
  • Leads come in, but there’s no consistent way to follow up and track them.
