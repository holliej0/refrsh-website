About

Design and sales, under one roof.

REFRSH is a small, husband-and-wife team based in New Jersey. Hollie leads design and customer experience. James leads business development. Together we cover the whole path from first impression to signed proposal.

Why we started

Good companies were getting overlooked.

We kept running into the same kind of business. Good people, good work, happy customers, and a website that didn’t do any of it justice. New business mostly came from word of mouth, and the bigger opportunities went to competitors who simply looked more ready.

Fixing that takes two skills that rarely sit in the same place: designing a clear, credible experience, and actually going out and starting conversations with buyers. That’s why we built REFRSH as a pair.

The name is simple. We help businesses refresh how they show up, and we keep things lean while we do it.
